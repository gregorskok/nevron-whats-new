Guests can now pay by card in the app. Built over six weeks: secure card checkout (Stripe), receipts, refunds down to a single item, dispute handling, settlement reports, and per-dashboard rules for when online payment is offered instead of charge-to-room.

Part II · The last two weeks

What's new right now

26 June – 10 July 2026 · 39 updates merged

The regular bi-weekly digest begins here: the freshest changes, each with its plain-language deep-dive.

The big story
In-room TV

The room TV is becoming a full service channel

Until now, guests could browse on the TV but had to reach for their phone to actually do anything. This cycle changes that: the TV got a full “My orders & reservations” screen, a live view of the room bill, and a cart & checkout redesigned for remote-control navigation.

Reservations made from the TV now respect each hotel's booking rules — past time slots are blocked, and the confirmation-PIN limit is applied per hotel instead of globally. Room Care requests from the TV (towels, cleaning, maintenance) are finished and ready to deploy.

What guests will notice

Guest app

Free items can finally be ordered

Items without a price — a complimentary newspaper, extra pillows — used to hide their order button entirely. They now show it and go through checkout as 0 €, so hotels can offer free services through the same flow.

Guest app

Images load noticeably faster

Photos and banners are now served from a fast cache instead of going through the backend on every view, and common banner sizes are pre-generated when a hotel is set up. Guests see content sooner, especially on slow hotel Wi-Fi.

What hotel staff will notice

Admin

Merge duplicate guest records — with undo

When the same guest exists twice (a PMS import plus a manual entry), staff can now merge them into one profile. Merged the wrong pair? One click undoes it. Manual links between records stay put on future syncs.

Admin

Stays list shows correct room occupancy

The guest count per room could disagree with what the in-room TV showed. The list now counts occupants the same way everywhere and sorts stays with the nearest arrival first.

One currency setting for the whole hotel

Three overlapping currency settings could disagree — the TV could show € while the settings said £, and some screens fell back to a hardcoded euro symbol. Now the hotel's Default Currency is set once and every price everywhere follows it, with a per-item exception staff can enable when a hotel truly needs it.

Under the hood

Invisible, but you'll feel it

Platform

A whole floor of TVs can reboot at once

After a power cut, dozens of TVs waking up together used to overwhelm the system — some failed with token errors and needed manual attention. TVs now share the heavy startup work and retry politely, so mass reboots just work.

PR #429Monday card
Platform

Speed & housekeeping across the platform

Database shortcuts (indexes) were added on the busiest queries, repeated-query hotspots were cached, and deleting a hotel now properly removes all its cloud files — four sources of “orphaned” storage were closed.
