In-room TVAdmin panelIn QA

The room TV gets games, music groups, film search and favourites

This cycle the room TV got the things guests reach for in the evening. There are eight built-in games, music sorted into groups, a film search on the remote, and favourite channels. Most of it was built for the first offline cruise ship, so it matches what the older Axon system offered. Every hotel that uses Nevron set-top boxes gets it too.

The games (Snake, Solitaire, Sudoku, 2048, Memory, Word search, Swarm and Five) run inside the TV app with no internet. They show on Nevron boxes and PC only. Samsung, LG, VIDAA, Philips and Android TVs hide them. New hotels get a “Games” category automatically.

The TV also remembers the guest's choices for the stay: the last channel, the channel group, and the sound language for films. A heart button on the control row adds a channel to the guest's favourites, and CH+/CH− then steps through favourites only.
