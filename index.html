Guests with a diet or an allergy get answers they can trust

This cycle every dish can carry its allergens and diet labels, and the guest sees them in the app, on the TV and in the chat. The rule is simple: the AI suggests, staff confirm. Only tags a staff member confirmed ever reach a guest.

Each dish in Catalogues and Room Care can hold the 14 EU allergens (contains or may contain) and six diet labels: vegetarian, vegan, halal-suitable, gluten-free, contains pork and contains alcohol. The AI reads the dish text and drafts the tags. Staff check them on a new admin page, Allergens & diet. Risky dishes are opened one by one. If someone edits the dish text, the tags go back for a new check.

The same care now covers uploaded PDF menus. Dishes inside a PDF get tags too, and the guest chat runs in a careful mode for a guest with a diet or allergy. It offers only confirmed dishes that fit, and it never says “free of”, “nut-free” or “safe”. A priced PDF section with no recorded dishes (a spa price list, for example) stays hidden from these guests until staff confirm “Nothing to eat or drink here”.
