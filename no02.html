The big story
Guest appIn QA

Guests can now watch series in the guest app

Movies got a sibling. A brand-new Series module landed this cycle: hotels curate shows, and guests browse them on a redesigned browse screen, open a series page with its seasons and episode lists, and play episodes right inside the guest app — in the hotel's branding, on the guest's own device.

The app remembers where each guest stopped, so an episode paused at bedtime resumes the next evening. Every episode also has its own direct link, which means a QR code on a flyer or a promo banner can jump straight into a specific episode. And following our analytics-from-day-one rule, the module shipped with its own dashboard card — plays, watch progress, top titles — so hotels can see what's actually being watched.

Order on the TV, pay on your phone

TV checkout now shows a QR code: the guest scans it, the hotel's payment page opens on their phone, they pay by card there, and the TV confirms the order is paid. The last missing step of TV ordering — payment — no longer needs a call to reception.

Under the hood

Invisible, but you'll feel it

Platform

Outage-proofing the image pipeline

A run of production outages was traced to images: when a small preview was missing, the server streamed the huge original instead and ran itself out of memory. That's fixed — and images across the guest app, admin panel and TV now come from a fast delivery network, with every hotel-creation path stocking it correctly.

Platform

A near-miss became a platform-wide security rule

During review of the TV pay-by-phone feature, a subtle flaw was caught before merge: a payment code from one hotel could, under the right timing, have been replayed at a different hotel. It was fixed the same day — and generalized into a written rule every future feature is checked against.
